# Cross-occurrence effect trigger audit

Status: audit complete; one confirmed preservation defect awaits disposition.
Code baseline: `3a701617`. Audit date: 2026-10-03.
No production changes are part of this investigation.

## Question and evidence bounds

Which currently modeled effects carry consequences beyond acquisition, what
supported edit changes their actual consumer, and does that consumer own an
independent authored outcome? Carryover alone does not justify extending the
Hermes, Supply Chain, Steady Growth and Transcendent Embryo settlement policy.

The durable inventory baseline is
`docs/audits/rooms-and-routes/SCHEDULED_AND_AUTOMATIC_TIMELINE_OUTCOMES_AUDIT.md`.
Current contracts are owned by `docs/design/REWARD_MODEL.md`,
`docs/design/AUTHORED_PROJECT_MODEL.md`, and
`docs/design/ROOM_LIFECYCLE_MODEL.md`. This investigation combines current code,
existing focused tests and supported-command fixture probes. It is not new native
or live-game verification. It covers modeled consequences, not every numeric
Hades II effect or future catalog addition.

World Shops have declaration-bound host/profile constraints. A hypothetical
World-Shop-to-World-Shop replacement is not evidence for relocation behavior.
Gold's one-use consumption occurs when its duplicate materializes, before the
source acquisition's children settle. Removing consumption is therefore distinct
from replacing a room or editing a downstream acquisition result.

Clearing all qualifying commerce triggers leaves unused refill details dormant.
Retained refill purchases or Gold pickup actions can still require repair.
Derived inventory does not authorize automatic purchases, and dormant data does
not manufacture an active refill. These are existing retention contracts, not
missing four-effect reconciliation.

## Confirmed defect: Rivals drops boss Arcana results

Supported-command probe:

1. Load the Underworld F/G fixture and equip Crystal Figurine.
2. Use the candidate session to choose legal `ChanneledCast` and `HealthRegen`
   through `ReplaceFigurineArcana`.
3. Simulation reports F valid, no findings, and `temporaryArcanaActivated` at
   `golden-f-preboss-shop:boss / Encounter`.
4. Set `BossDifficultyShrineUpgrade` to rank 1 through `ReplaceFearVowRank`.
5. The same occurrence changes F_Boss01 to F_Boss02. Simulation now reports
   `figurineOutcomeMissing`, required 2, selected 0, at the same owner.

Both declarations use the Boss template, SingleEncounter and fixed Encounter
slot. The pending source and boss-defeated outcome contact still apply.
`authored-project/commands/project-state.ts::reconcileCompletionBosses` calls
`authored-project/room-state/encounter-reconciliation.ts::reconcileRoomEncounterState`,
whose reconstructed object omits both `judgmentArcanaKeysByPhase` and
`figurineArcanaKeysByPhase`. A separate supported-command probe established both
maps are dropped; active-result validation was demonstrated for Figurine.
No located authority permits this reset.

Recommended scope is preserving these fixed-contact authored maps on compatible
boss reconstruction, with owning command regressions and ordinary downstream
candidate validation. Do not use this defect to introduce blanket source-identity
resets or relocate consumer-owned observations.

## Evidence and remaining bounds

Focused suites passed:

- 133 commerce engine/UI tests, plus trigger-removal probes for Gold and all
  three Travel Deal domains.
- 67 Artificer, Time Piece, Concave Stone and Calling Card tests.
- 53 Echo Gift, Echo replay and Stygian Well tests. This overlaps commerce
  coverage; counts are not a unique repository-wide total.

Primary implementation anchors, relative to `packages/planner-engine/src/`:

- `simulation/rewards/shop/{settlement,derived-rewards}.ts`;
  `simulation/commerce/{hermes-shrine,stygian-well}.ts`.
- `simulation/rewards/biome/lifecycle-transitions/echo-keepsake-replay.ts`;
  `authored-project/commands/keepsake.ts`.
- `authored-project/chaos-gate-reconciliation.ts` and
  `authored-project/commands/route-detours.ts`.
- `authored-project/acquisition/{sea-star,pickup-producers}.ts`.
- `simulation/traits/history/fold.ts` and encounter-end effects.

Narrow coverage gaps are not confirmed defects: same-kind Echo result reuse
through a remove/reinstate command sequence, and richly authored Ixion subtree
loss during host reassignment. Existing behavior is explicit biome ownership and
default regeneration respectively; changing either requires a policy decision.

The old All Together/shop source audit's claim that Wells are unsupported is
stale relative to implemented Well behavior and the Room Features audit. Native
consumable-restock timing and precise Shrine refill spawn contact remain bounded
source questions; planner probes do not resolve them.

Retire this investigation after disposition of the boss-preservation defect and
any explicitly selected remaining probes. Promote only new durable facts or
policy to their existing authorities. Future modeled families need their own
trigger/owner disposition rather than inheriting one universal relocation rule.
