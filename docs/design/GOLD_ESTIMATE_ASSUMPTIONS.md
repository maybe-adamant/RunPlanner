# Gold Estimate Assumptions

The [gold ledger](REWARD_MODEL.md#gold-ledger) is exact wherever the game's gold
is fixed or a bounded roll. Where the game leaves gold to how the player plays
or to rosters the plan does not fix, it uses the assumptions and tuning values
below. Each value has one row: what it means, why it has its value, and how to
re-tune it. Code names the row it implements and does not restate the reason.

Re-tuning from recorded runs: `node scripts/gold-calibration.mjs <raw save
folder>...` prints each payout group's observed store and gold paid per room
(it needs a dev build's Record mode saves). Re-check the source rows against
the game scripts [when the game updates](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#when-the-game-updates).

cells and paid fractions are given at the native drop chance, then at an
enemy gold drop chance of 25, 50, 75 and 100%; spreads are standard
deviations before the money multiplier; kills are expected kills at Hordes 0.

## Approximation

| Assumption               | Value                                     | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                             | Rationale and evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Re-tune |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Normal approximation     | mean and variance                         | The balance is summed as independent terms' moments and read as normal                                                                                                              | Many independent rooms; rolled prices and sale values are uniform on their step                                                                                                                                                                 | –       |
| Continuity correction    | +0.5                                      | P(balance − price ≥ 0) reads the normal at mean + 0.5                                                                                                                               | Gold is whole                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | –       |
| Estimate bounds          | ±3 sd, floored at 0                       | A gain's shown range                                                                                                                                                                | Display only                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | –       |
| Expected kills for Medea | real-valued                               | Medea's capped drops read the room's expected kills as a binomial over a real count                                                                                                 | Unknown rosters and hatching give expected, not whole, kills                                                                                                                                                                                    | –       |
| Coin spawn contacts      | screen close; room entry or encounter end | A trait's coins are valued as its screen closes; a room reward's at its room's entry (cages, optionals) or latest encounter end (incoming reward); stores at the door into the room | The game values coins at spawn (`RoomLogic.lua:1944-1985`, `TraitLogic.lua:1045-1058`); the walk takes the nearest contact it sees. A reward collected in another room, and coins whose spawn is not walked, read the multipliers at collection | –       |
