# Snap mode live acceptance

Turn on **Snap back to the plan** and play a plan. Desync on purpose in an
ordinary room, then take any door: you enter the planned next room and the
header returns to Synchronized. Check each item in that snapped room unless it
says otherwise.

## Hero state after a snap

- [ ] Re-rank a keepsake at a rack, then snap: its counters are right, the
      Silver Wheel max-Magick grant is right, Silken Sash armor is kept, and
      Reincarnation and cat Death Defiance pips are as before the snap.
- [ ] Take or drop a temporary Arcana card, then snap: the card set,
      `TemporaryMetaUpgrades` and Equipped flags match the plan. Re-enable a
      vow Circe disabled: the damage modifier and the biome timer return.
- [ ] Invest a different Path of Stars node, then snap: the talent tree shows
      the planned nodes, point counts and talent stacks.
- [ ] Force an essence or boon element difference, including one that needs
      an Elemental essence split: element totals match after the snap.
- [ ] The trait tray and Boon list show the snapped build with no stale icons.
- [ ] Play two more rooms after a snap: no door or reward proof mismatches on
      run records or reward stores.
- [ ] Save & Quit after a snap, then reload: traits and maxima persist, and the
      run continues unsteered.

## Declines and integrity

- [ ] A wrong turn into Ephyra's board, or across a biome edge, stays out of
      sync and does not snap.
- [ ] After a victory with snaps, the summary shows "Snapped back N times".

## Store markers

With **Highlight planned choices** on:

- [ ] A Hermes Shrine with a planned purchase and rush marks only the next one,
      and its marker sits clear of the rush sticker.
- [ ] A Purging Pool with a planned sale marks only that trait's row, whatever
      its position in the trait tray.
